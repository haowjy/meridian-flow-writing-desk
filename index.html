The editor stays home, with sources and files attached to it. The question is where Work and team management live.

Keep the manuscript home; open Work as a project utility and manage the team inside Chat.

What this costs: Calmest writing home, with one extra step to configuration.

Phone

Give sustained planning a real Write / Work switch; Chat stays beside either destination.

What this costs: Better management focus, but more navigation away from prose.

Desktop

Put goals, running agents, review and tasks in Chat Activity; Team remains a child of Chat.

What this costs: Fewest surfaces, but Chat must carry both conversation and coordination.

Desktop

Phone

Make agent and skill configuration a dedicated Studio beside Write; keep Work a drawer.

What this costs: Management is discoverable, but an infrequent activity earns permanent chrome.

Desktop

Group Work, Team and Skills inside one project-scoped Room utility, outside Chat.

What this costs: One home for project operations; Room needs discipline to avoid becoming a junk drawer.

Desktop

Give Sources and Files a roomier material column; keep A’s nesting for Chat, Work and Team.

What this costs: Better browsing and context, at the cost of manuscript width.
